# Refund policy

Company approval recorded for the current V1 launch documents.

COMPANY APPROVAL = CONFIRMED for the controlled limited beta. This is not legal counsel approval and it is not regulatory approval.

ERAP version 1 has no automatic refund engine. A refund request is handled manually by an authorized support or admin process. The payment provider's record remains authoritative. ERAP does not invent a refund webhook and does not treat a payment as a refund.

Support must not ask for a card number, a password, a one-time code, or a payment secret. Identify the organization, the correlation id, and the billing entity id.

Company approval of this V1 text is recorded for the controlled limited beta. Legal counsel approval is not claimed.
