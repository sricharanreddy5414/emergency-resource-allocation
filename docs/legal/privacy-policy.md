# Privacy policy

V1 DRAFT — REQUIRES FINAL COMPANY/LEGAL APPROVAL

This draft describes the information ERAP stores so an organization can operate. It is not a lawyer-approved privacy policy, and it does not name a registered company, address, or regulator.

ERAP stores the account identity supplied by the sign-in service, organization membership, and the operational records that members create: locations, resources, requests, allocations, exchange activity, notifications, and billing state. Billing uses a payment provider. ERAP does not ask people to send card numbers, passwords, one-time codes, or payment secrets to support.

Support identifies a problem with an organization id, a correlation id, and an entity id. Tenant records stay inside the organization that owns them.

This draft does not claim a completed privacy review. Final company or legal approval is still required before customer launch.
