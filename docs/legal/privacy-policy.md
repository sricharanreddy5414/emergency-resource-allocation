# Privacy policy

Company approval recorded for the current V1 launch documents.

COMPANY APPROVAL = CONFIRMED for the controlled limited beta. This is not legal counsel approval and it is not regulatory approval. The document does not name a registered company, address, or regulator.

This page describes the information ERAP stores so an organization can operate. It is not a lawyer-approved privacy policy.

ERAP stores the account identity supplied by the sign-in service, organization membership, and the operational records that members create: locations, resources, requests, allocations, exchange activity, notifications, and billing state. Billing uses a payment provider. ERAP does not ask people to send card numbers, passwords, one-time codes, or payment secrets to support.

Support identifies a problem with an organization id, a correlation id, and an entity id. Tenant records stay inside the organization that owns them.

This page does not claim a completed privacy review by legal counsel. Company approval of this V1 text is recorded for the controlled limited beta.
